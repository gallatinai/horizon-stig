## Source differences retained

The Oct 26 notes required ASD review and described UDS selection as uncertain in the accompanying draft. The Nov 6 scope describes UDS shipped with Navigator and asserts ASD inheritance. This repository follows that newer intended scope while preserving the older rule-level analysis as historical context. MPN is its own assess-only deployment path; do not treat the separate DU/2F ATO package as proof that MPN requirements are satisfied.

The Nov 6 scope lists self-hosted S3 and model serving, whereas the inspected bundle remains a general candidate with external LLM egress. That is recorded as a profile-selection/integration requirement rather than quietly assessing the wrong architecture.
